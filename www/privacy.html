Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Fortuna Coach (“Fortuna”, “we”) is an investing education and paper-trading practice app. This policy describes what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account email and password (handled by Supabase Auth)
  • App progress: lessons, quiz results, paper-trading balances and trades
  • Pro status if you subscribe on the website

We do not connect to a real brokerage. Balances and prices in the app are simulated.

How we use it

  • To sign you in on the website and the iPhone app
  • To save your progress across devices
  • To unlock Pro after a web subscription

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Service providers we use: Supabase (accounts and database) and Stripe (website payments only). Payments are not processed inside the iPhone app.

Your choices

You can log out, reset the practice account, or email us to delete your account.
